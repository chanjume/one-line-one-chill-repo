# One Line, One Chill

A single sentence of horror, written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.ts`
(Node 22, no dependencies).

> The doorbell camera showed me unlocking our front door at 11:40 p.m., twenty minutes before I actually left the office.

119 characters, one sentence. The scare is earned, not announced: a
doorbell camera, a timestamp, and an alibi that proves the person at the
door was not me.
